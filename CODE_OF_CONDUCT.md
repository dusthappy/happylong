# 贡献者公约行为准则 / Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our pledge

我们承诺为每个人营造无骚扰的参与环境，不因年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人形象、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待。

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺以有助于开放、友善、多元、包容和健康社区的方式行事与互动。

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 我们的标准 / Our standards

积极行为包括： / Positive behavior includes:

- 展现同理心与善意； / demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- 尊重不同观点与经验； / respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- 提出并优雅接受建设性反馈； / giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- 承担责任、向受影响者道歉并从经验中学习； / accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from mistakes;
- 关注对整个社区最有利的事情。 / focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

不可接受的行为包括： / Unacceptable behavior includes:

- 性化语言、意象或不受欢迎的性关注； / sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损评论以及人身或政治攻击； / trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- 公开或私下骚扰； / public or private harassment;
- 未经明确许可发布他人的住址、电子邮箱等私人信息； / publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- 其他可被合理认定为不适合职业环境的行为。 / other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 执行职责 / Enforcement responsibilities

维护者负责解释并执行可接受行为标准，并会对不当、威胁、冒犯或有害行为采取适当且公平的纠正措施。维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 与其他贡献，并在适当时说明处理理由。

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本准则适用于所有项目空间，也适用于个人在公共场合正式代表本项目时的行为。

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## 执行 / Enforcement

请通过维护者 GitHub 资料页列出的私密渠道报告辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受行为。若没有可用的私密渠道，可使用本仓库 **Security** 标签页的私密报告入口，并明确标注为行为准则事件。所有报告都会被及时、公平地审查；维护者会尽力保护报告者的隐私与安全。

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, use the private reporting entry under this repository's **Security** tab and identify the report as a Code of Conduct incident. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with the reporter's privacy and safety protected as far as possible.

执行分级如下： / Enforcement levels:

1. **警告 / Warning**：首次或轻微违规，要求停止并说明影响。 / For a first or minor violation, require the behavior to stop and explain its impact.
2. **临时封禁 / Temporary ban**：警告后继续违规，或发生持续骚扰、人身攻击等较重行为时，在指定期限内禁止参与项目空间。 / Continued violations or serious conduct such as sustained harassment or personal attacks result in a time-limited ban from project spaces.
3. **永久封禁 / Permanent ban**：威胁、性骚扰、恶意破坏等严重违规或屡次违规者会被永久移出社区。 / Threats, sexual harassment, malicious disruption, or repeated violations result in permanent removal from the community.

安全漏洞请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 单独私密报告，不要与行为准则事件混用。

Report security vulnerabilities separately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not combine them with conduct incidents.
